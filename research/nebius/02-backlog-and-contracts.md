# Nebius Group N.V. (NBIS) — contracted revenue / backlog: what is publicly knowable

Compiled 2026-11-11 (the day before the Q3 2026 report). Latest reported quarter: Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-12).

## How to read the evidence labels

Every fact carries a source ID (see "Source list" at the end), a tier and a verification mark.

- Tier: **T1** Nebius SEC filing / press release / shareholder letter / earnings call; **T2** counterparty; **T3** financial or trade press; **T4** analyst blogs, opinion, social media.
- **[P]** = I read the primary document text in this session (SEC filing text from EDGAR; nebius.com press releases; data.sec.gov XBRL).
- **[T]** = earnings-call transcript read in full (Motley Fool transcription of a T1 call; transcription errors possible).
- **[S]** = rests only on a web-search result summary or snippet; the page itself was NOT opened. Treat as a lead.
- **INFERRED** = my own arithmetic or reasoning, not a company statement.

Access notes: SEC filings, nebius.com releases and Motley Fool transcripts were read directly. `assets.nebius.com` (PDF letters) was not reachable, so the SEC exhibit copies of the letters were used. The October–November 2026 press items (Microsoft capex cut, satellite analysis, Bloomberg report) could not be opened or cross-checked — those are all [S].

## 1. What Nebius discloses

### 1.1 Filing status

- Nebius is still a foreign private issuer: Form 20-F for FY2025 filed 2026-04-30 (20-F/A 2026-05-22), everything else on Form 6-K. No 10-K/10-Q/8-K. [P] SEC filing index (S23). T1.
- Since Q1 2026 each quarter produces two 6-Ks: (a) results press release + shareholder letter; (b) "Operating and Financial Review" + unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements with full notes (Q1 2026 filed 2026-05-20; Q2 2026 filed 2026-08-12). The notes are where RPO, deferred revenue and customer concentration sit. [P] S10, S11, S13.
- Insiders have filed Forms 3/4 since 2026-03-18. [P] S23.
- FY2025 was audited by Reanda Audit & Assurance B.V.; the board resolved to move to Deloitte for FY2026. Interim figures are unaudited. [P] S22.

### 1.3 Definitions (verbatim)

- ARR, 2024–2025: "ARR is defined as annualized run-rate revenue by the end of the period (revenue for last month of the period multiplied by twelve)." [P] N7 (2025-02-20), S19. T1.
- ARR, 2026: "Annualized run-rate (ARR) is calculated by taking Nebius AI cloud revenue from the last month of the quarter multiplied by 12." [P] S9 (2026-08-12), S12. T1. (Scope narrowed from group/"revenue" to "Nebius AI cloud revenue".)
- Power, 20-F FY2025: "Contracted power is capacity that has been secured by land and contracted power commitments. As of February 2026, we had contracted more than 2 GW of power." / "Connected power is capacity that has power connected into data centers" / "Active power is capacity being consumed by IT equipment and available for revenue generation. As of December 31, 2025, we had approximately 170 MW of active power capacity across the globe". [P] S1. T1.
- Power, Q1 2026 letter: "(1) contracted power, or power secured by contracted land and power commitments; (2) connected power, or power connected into fully built and equipped data centers; and (3) active power, or power being consumed by installed, operational IT equipment and available for revenue generation." [P] S12. T1.
- RPO: "Remaining performance obligations ("RPO") represent the transaction price allocated to performance obligations that are unsatisfied or partially unsatisfied as of the end of the reporting period. RPO consists of deferred revenue and unbilled contract revenue, which includes non-cancelable contracts where the Group has not yet invoiced, maintains an obligation to perform, and has not recognized revenue in the financial statements." [P] S1, S10. T1.
- RPO caveats: "The amounts disclosed include the Group's best estimate of variable consideration which is subject to change due to the timing and performance constraints. The amounts do not include performance obligations with an original duration of one year or less. The amounts are fully attributed to the Group's Nebius AI cloud business." [P] S10. T1.
- Deferred revenue: "The Group recognizes deferred revenue when cash is received and before performance obligations are fulfilled, including amounts that may be refundable. Deferred revenue balances primarily relate to strategic customer agreements." [P] S1, S10. T1.
- Revenue models: "We offer both on-demand "pay-as-you-go" pricing and fixed "reserved capacity" contracts." [P] S1, S11. T1.
- ACV / payback (Q2 2026 letter footnotes): "(1) ACV — Annual contract value. (2) Methodology is on a revenue recognition basis, excluding any prepayment. (3) Estimated payback period for new deals signed in Q2, based on forecast costs and contracted future capacity, including capacity not yet built." [P] S9. T1.

Dollar amounts are INFERRED from the rounded percentages. Verbatim (Q2 2026): "As of June 30, 2026, the amount of unsatisfied RPO was $37,490.6, of which 36% is expected to be recognized as revenue during the 24 months ending June 30, 2028, 40% between months 25 and 48, and the remainder recognized thereafter." Points that follow from the text:
- No next-12-months figure is disclosed. The nearest proxy is current deferred revenue ($979.4M at 2026-06-30), which understates 12-month recognition because monthly-invoiced fees are not in deferred revenue. INFERRED.
- Contracts with an original duration of one year or less are outside RPO, so pay-as-you-go usage and the new 3–6 month "short-term capacity" deals never appear in it. [P] S10.
- "Deferred revenue is expected to be recognized as revenue over the period ranging from one to five calendar years." [P] S10.
- RPO grew $12.25B in Q1 2026, which matches the $12B firm part of the March 2026 Meta agreement; the $15B "unsold capacity" order therefore appears not to be in RPO. INFERRED.
- At 2025-12-31 Microsoft ($17,392.9M) plus Meta #1 ($2,880.7M) = $20,273.6M against RPO of $21,333.0M, so the two hyperscaler contracts were about 95% of RPO at year-end 2025. INFERRED (ignores the small amount already recognized in Q4 2025).
- Management's ">$40 billion" of investment-grade commitments exceeds total GAAP RPO ($37.5B, all customers). It can only be reached by counting items outside RPO, most plausibly the $15B Meta backstop (firm investment-grade TCV without it is about $32B–$34B). INFERRED; Nebius has not reconciled the two figures.

### 2.2 Management statements on delivery and ramp (T1)

Microsoft:
- "The Microsoft GPU Services are being deployed in nine tranches during 2025 and 2026 … including aggregate upfront payments of approximately $6,958.1, with the remaining consideration invoiced monthly over the service terms of the respective tranches through October 2031." [P] S1 (figures in $ millions). Upfront = 40.0% of $17,392.9M, INFERRED.
- Addendum No. 1 (2026-01-21) added "2 additional GPU tranches" with no upfront payment (dates and value redacted). [P] S5.
- "We delivered the first tranche in November last year… it ramps up starting from the midyear and most of the volumes will be coming in Q3 and Q4." Delivery schedule runs "up to the end of this year". [T] F2 (2026-05-13).
- "Nebius recently delivered the latest planned capacity tranche to Microsoft, and remains on track to deliver the remaining tranches consistent with the terms of the contracted schedule." [P] N10 (2026-07-17).
- "To date, we've delivered all capacity tranches to Microsoft under the contract, including two tranches over the past month." [P] S9 (2026-08-12). On the call: "we delivered all the tranches that we were required to deliver under the contract up to date." [T] F1.
- Number of the 11 tranches delivered: not disclosed (first Nov 2025, second Feb 2026, and at least two more in the month before 2026-08-12, so at least four — INFERRED; the tranche announced on 2026-07-17 may be one of those two).

Meta:
- Meta #1: two tranches "during December 2025 and February 2026" (6-K); "we are now fully in the servicing stage" (2026-02-12). [P] S7, S14.
- Meta #2: $12B dedicated capacity "with deployments in tranches starting early 2027" (6-K) / "throughout the first half of 2027" (20-F); buildout "on track to come online in early 2027" (2026-08-12). [P] S8, S1, S9.

AI-cloud deals and capacity:
- "The majority of these deals were signed against capacity arriving in late 2026, and will contribute primarily to 2027 revenue." [P] S9.
- "Both deal terms I just described are all coming online later this year. So they will not have a material effect on our 2026 revenue guidance. However… they will definitely affect our 2027 revenue and beyond." (Volozh) [T] F1.
- "expect capacity deployed late in Q2 to begin contributing to revenue in Q3." (Alonso) [T] F1.
- "there is a gap between the connected power and the revenues coming… that takes a few months"; the 800 MW–1 GW is "true about the connected, but I would think that about being active in — throughout the first half of 2027." (Korolenko) [T] F1.
- 5 GW contracted: "Almost all of that power will come online over the next 3, maybe to 3.5 years". [T] F1.
- 2027: "we plan to deploy more than 1 GW per year, starting in 2027"; "We are going to deploy significantly more capacity in 2027"; formal 2027 guidance "later this year". [P] S9; [T] F1.
- Sites: Missouri and Alabama "operational in 2027"; Pennsylvania first 250–300 MW by end-2027, +300 MW a year to 1.2 GW by 2030. [P] S12; [T] F2.

INFERRED cross-check: H1 2026 revenue was $981.3M, so the $3.0B–$3.4B guide needs about $2.0B–$2.4B in H2. The 0–24 month RPO bucket ($13.5B to June 2028) is about what a flat $7B run-rate would produce over that window; anything above that has to come from new, renewed, short-term or on-demand business.

### Split between long-term hyperscaler contracts and the AI cloud

- Revenue split: not disclosed.
- Three deal types as described on 2026-08-12: 3–6 month short-term; "Mid-term contracts — our core business — now with an average duration of one to three years"; "Long-term contracts with investment-grade customers, which help us finance our buildout faster and more efficiently." [P] S9.
- "the vast majority of our contracts is the cloud contracts, where we have the flexibility where within the region, we can deliver it, reducing the dependency on any one location." (Korolenko) [T] F1.
- "The vast majority of capacity coming online over the next several quarters to 12 months is already under contract or earmarked for our AI cloud customers. We do retain a portion of capacity for self-service". (Boroditsky, 2026-05-13) [T] F2.
- By value, the contracted base is dominated by two investment-grade customers (about 95% of RPO at end-2025; INFERRED, §2.1).

## 6. Conditions that void or reduce a contract — verbatim

### 6.1 Microsoft (most complete public record)

Take-or-pay — SOW §1.1(c): "Microsoft acknowledges and agrees that, by virtue of this reservation, it is obligated to pay the Fees for each GPU Service accepted in accordance with Section 2 for the entirety of its Service Term, irrespective of the actual usage or consumption of such GPU Service by Microsoft." [P] S4.

No termination for convenience — SOW §1.1(a): the Agreement "may be terminated solely in accordance with Section 3.3 of this SOW and Section 14 of Exhibit F." [P] S4.

Late delivery, as summarised by Nebius — 6-K 2025-09-08: "In the event that, following a grace period, Company fails to meet agreed delivery dates for a GPU Service and the Company cannot provide alternative capacity, Microsoft has the right to terminate that GPU Service." [P] S6.

Late delivery, contract text — SOW §3.3:
- "(b) If a GPU Service is not Accepted by Microsoft by the end of its applicable Acceptance Period, then a credit of Liquidated Damages (as described in sub-section c below) shall be applied from the day after the applicable, failed Acceptance Period."
- "(c) The Liquidated Damages shall when payable for a GPU Service in accordance with sub-section b above, equal, for each day of delay, [*] of the daily bill for each day until the GPU Service is successfully delivered, or subject to sub-section d below."
- "(d) If: (i) a GPU Service has not been Accepted within [*] days following the end of the applicable Acceptance Period (the "Grace Period"); or (ii) Nebius's notifications to Microsoft make clear that a GPU Service, in Microsoft's reasonable judgment, is not likely to be Accepted within the Grace Period, and Nebius cannot deliver an equivalent capacity (as determined by Microsoft in its sole reasonable discretion) by the Delivery Date, Microsoft may elect to either terminate the applicable GPU Service or agree to an updated Delivery Date (the "Updated Delivery Date"), such election to be exercised by no later than the final day of the Grace Period. Liquidated Damages shall no longer be payable after the Grace Period in respect of the relevant GPU Services. If Microsoft elects to terminate the applicable GPU Service, Nebius shall refund the Upfront Payment paid by Microsoft for such GPU Service, less any Liquidated Damages Paid in relation to such GPU Service."
- "(e) If Microsoft has agreed to an Updated Delivery Date under Section 3.3.c and the applicable GPU Service is not delivered on or before the Updated Delivery Date, the applicable GPU Service will be deemed terminated by Microsoft, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing."
- "(f) If a specific GPU Service is terminated by Microsoft in accordance with its right in this Section 3.3, Microsoft will have no obligation to pay for that GPU Service."
- "(h) Nebius will not deliver a tranche until all tranches with an earlier Delivery Date have been successfully merged with the existing production tranche(s)…" (a slip in one tranche can hold up the next; this sub-section does not apply to the two added tranches). [P] S4, S5.

Reading: the remedy is per tranche, not the whole contract; the trigger is acceptance, not shipment; the grace-period length and damages rate are redacted; the upfront payment for a terminated tranche is refundable.

Partial acceptance — SOW §2: "Microsoft will Accept any GPU Service with a minimum of [*]% of the GPUs being functional." [P] S4.

Termination for cause and insolvency — 6-K: "Either party may terminate the Agreement for cause due to the other party's material breach (if not remedied within 60 days); or (ii) if the other party ceases doing business, terminates its existence, dissolves or liquidates, or proceedings are instituted by or against it under any bankruptcy or insolvency law (which proceedings are not dismissed within 30 days), provided that such right of termination shall not arise where a party has entered into Chapter 11 Bankruptcy and is undertaking reorganization or transfer activities…" [P] S6.

Change of control — SOW Exhibit F §14(b): "In the event of a proposed Change of Control of Nebius to which Microsoft has not granted its consent pursuant to Section 22, Microsoft shall have the right to terminate this Agreement"; ""Restricted Party" means Amazon or Google." [P] S4.

Availability — 20-F: "The agreement includes service-level commitments and provides Microsoft with rights to receive service credits or to terminate individual tranches in the event of specified delivery delays or repeated failure to meet availability requirements." The SLA itself is not public. Addendum 1 adds: "the Monthly Uptime Percentage for the first tranche will be [*] until [*]. The credit for failure to meet this uptime percentage will be [*]". [P] S1, S5.

Liability cap — Exhibit F §19(d): liability "shall be limited to, as a total aggregate cap, one tenth (1/10) of the total Service Fees for the GPU Services". [P] S4.

Site — SOW §1.1(a): services are provided "at the Data Center Location. The Data Center Location shall be the data center facilities located at [*]." Shareholder letter: Microsoft "will be served exclusively from our New Jersey data center." Substitute capacity counts only if Microsoft, "in its sole reasonable discretion", deems it equivalent. [P] S4, S15.

Start condition (financing link) — SOW §1.1A(e): "neither party will have any obligations under the Agreement until either of the Completion Scenarios… are satisfied"; scenarios are that Nebius obtains "the financing related to this Agreement" or "can finance this Agreement's capital expenditure without the need for financing". If neither occurred within [*] days the Agreement "shall be considered not to have come into effect". Deliveries from November 2025 imply this was met (INFERRED). [P] S4.

Pledge of cash flows — Exhibit F §26(b): Nebius "may pledge, assign, collaterally assign, hypothecate, sell, convey, or grant a lien or security interest in its right, title and interest in and to the Agreement, the receivables and other payments due hereunder". Microsoft may assign only to a party with "an investment grade credit rating". [P] S4.

Expansion mechanics — SOW §1.1(e): if the power reserve is not needed, "the total Service Fees would be estimated to increase from $[*] up to $[*]" (the $17.4B to $19.4B step, INFERRED). §1.2: right of first offer on future GPU capacity at the site. [P] S4.

Interim-report gloss — Q3 2025 note: "The Group must deliver capacity per specified timelines; failure may result in termination after a 60-day cure period… Standard termination rights exist for material breach or force majeure." [P] S16.

Termination for cause — Agreement §6.2.1–6.2.2: "either Party may terminate an Order if (i) the other Party fails to cure any material breach by such other Party of that Order, as applicable, within [*] days after written notice…"; "a material breach relating to a Tranche permits termination of that Tranche only and not any other Tranche or the applicable Order or this Agreement, except as may be provided in the applicable Order." ## 8. Outlook

Guidance in force (reaffirmed 2026-08-12; no later company release that I read, through 2026-10-30, changes it): year-end 2026 ARR $7B–$9B; 2026 revenue $3.0B–$3.4B; group adjusted EBITDA margin about 40%; capex $20B–$25B; 5 GW contracted and 800 MW–1 GW connected power by year-end; more than $9B of customer prepayments in 2026. [P] S9, N15; [T] F1.

2027 and beyond (no formal guidance yet):
- "We could sell our entire 2027 capacity on these terms today. We are deliberately not doing so because we see higher value in retaining some capacity for immediate customer needs." [P] S9.
- "our model was built to build capacity in advance, but not to presell it in advance." (Volozh) [T] F1.
- "We have also… tactically shortened how far in advance we sell capacity." (Boroditsky) [T] F1.
- Q2 deals "with more than $20 million per megawatt… and less than 2-year payback period… start coming online from late Q4 and onwards. This can serve as the baseline for pricing early next year". (Alonso) [T] F1.
- "If the market remains strong, we should generate more than $27 billion in revenue from this great agreement." (Meta #2) [T] F2.
- More large contracts: "I'm happy to announce the first of these contracts, and I believe there are more to come" (2025-09-08) [P] N8; "we may enter into significant long-term deals, provided they come with favorable unit economics" (2026-02-12) [P] S14; "with $40 billion in contracted backlog, we will do more of this [asset-backed borrowing]" (2026-08-12) [T] F1. No new hyperscaler contract has been announced since March 2026.

Grow, be replaced, or reduce — my reading (INFERRED):
1. The investment-grade base is fixed-size and amortises. Microsoft bills through October 2031; Meta #1 runs to about 2030–31; the $12B Meta #2 tranche runs roughly 2027–2032. Growth inside these contracts needs explicit options: Microsoft's reserve (to about $19.4B) and right of first offer, Meta #1's extension right, Meta #2's backstop.
2. The $15B Meta backstop is a floor, not growth. If Nebius sells that capacity to others as intended, Meta buys nothing under it.
3. Reported growth in contracted value now depends on 1–3 year AI-cloud contracts, which roll off and must be renewed or replaced on that cycle, and on 3–6 month deals that never enter RPO. Management is deliberately shortening duration to capture price. That raises near-term yield and lowers visibility.
4. Timing: about 36% of RPO converts by mid-2028 and about 76% by mid-2030. Late-2026 capacity produces 2027 revenue.
5. The main reduction path visible in the filed text is late delivery of Microsoft tranches at a single site, plus availability credits. Nothing public shows this has been triggered.

Critical analyses (opinion, T4 unless noted):
- Motley Fool, D. Sparks, 2026-10-19: RPO of $37.5B is "a little lower" than the ">$40 billion" management cites; "just 36%" converts in two years; three customers were 59% of Q2 revenue; H2 revenue must be "more than twice the first half's total". [P] F6.
- Motley Fool, D. Sparks, 2026-11-05: September ARR "around $4.6 billion would keep Nebius on track for the low end"; $12B–$17B of capex left for H2; the Citi facility "lasts for just 364 days". [P] F5.
- Motley Fool, J. Rice, 2026-11-09: consensus Q3 revenue "$908 million"; political and borrowing-cost risk to data-center build-outs; Reuters reported Nebius considered and passed on a high-yield bond. [P] F4.
- DA Davidson (G. Luria), 2026-08-10: Vineland may not complete in 2026. [S] S-f.
- Hunterbrook, August 2026: "Nebius Said Vineland 'On Track' Days After Stop-Work Orders". [S] S-e.
- btw.media, March 2026: the Meta figure is "$12bn of firm capacity plus a $15bn backstop". [S] S-l.
