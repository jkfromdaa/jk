## How to read this file

**Evidence labels used on every fact**

- **[P]** = I opened the primary document and read the text myself (SEC exhibit text, nebius.com page, or Motley Fool call transcript).
- **[S]** = rests on a web-search result summary only; the underlying page was NOT opened (DCD is bot-blocked, most trade press was not fetched).
- Tier: **T1** Nebius filing / press release / letter / call; **T2** counterparty; **T3** trade or local press; **T4** analyst blog, tracker, social.
- **INFERRED** = my own deduction, not stated by any source.

**Access notes.** Primary documents were read directly from SEC EDGAR, nebius.com and Motley Fool call transcripts. assets.nebius.com (shareholder-letter PDFs) and docs.nebius.com were not reachable; the letters were read from the SEC exhibit versions instead, whose graphics (site map) are garbled text.

**MW basis.** Nebius reports power as "contracted", "connected" and "active" and does not state whether site figures are critical IT MW or gross utility MW. Unless noted, every MW below is the headline figure as published ("capacity of up to X MW"); **critical-IT vs gross is "not disclosed"** for every site except AIB CLT1 (50 MW critical IT against a 65 MW electric service agreement).
