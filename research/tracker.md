## Which of the three outcomes does the evidence show now?

The report's outcomes are continued scarcity, digestion, and financial stress, and they can overlap.

- **Scarcity:** supported. Rental indices rose after the report's cutoff, and both companies report higher contract prices.
- **Digestion:** no sign yet. It would show as several rental indices falling together and pre-leasing dropping.
- **Financial stress:** some signs since the cutoff. Neocloud shares and bonds fell sharply in October [S], CoreWeave said it will not issue unsecured debt at current yields [P], and Nebius took a 364-day bridge loan [P]. Offsetting that, CoreWeave's operating margin turned positive.

So the current reading is scarcity and financial stress at the same time.
